About UNICO · Kepong Menjalara

A face-shape-first hair salon in Kepong Menjalara, Kuala Lumpur

UNICO Hair Studio is an appointment-only hair salon in Bandar Sri Menjalara, Kepong, established in 2018. Instead of copying trending styles, our six stylists design each haircut, hair colour and perm around your face shape, hair texture and daily routine — so it still looks good when you blow-dry it yourself at home.

Services include women's and men's haircuts, hair colour and touch-up, digital and root perms, bleach & colour, keratin treatment, Milbon and Olaplex hair treatments, and La Biosthétique scalp treatment. Every price includes wash and consultation. Our second line, UNICO SKINLAB, offers Korean-technology facial treatments in the same building.

Located at G2, Menara Perniagaan Manjalara Dua (shoplot parking in front), we're open daily 11am–8pm except Tuesdays. Rated 4.7★ from 370+ Google reviews. Book via WhatsApp 012-350 0624.

有,全部价格都包含洗发和 Consultation。
